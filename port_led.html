Configure WS2812 status LEDs, one per controller port, chained on a single GPIO.
Only available on BlueRetro HW2 N64 and GameCube. Changes are previewed live, press Save to keep them after a reboot.


Chain order


For 10 seconds the 1st LED of the chain lights red, the 2nd green, the 3rd blue and the 4th yellow. Then select for each port the color it showed.
